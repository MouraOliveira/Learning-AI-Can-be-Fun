Perceptron Lab: from lines to a neural network

Draw lines, place labelled points and choose each point’s target class. The tool finds the smallest Heaviside network that separates them (one line = one neuron; two or more lines = hidden neurons plus an output neuron) and shows every equation and weight.

Line: drag to draw (max 5; the arrow points to the side where the neuron outputs 1, chosen automatically towards the class-1 points). Hover a line to read its start and end coordinates. The Snap menu lets lines start and end between integer coordinates. Point: click to place. Move: drag handles or points; click a point to flip its class. Right-click or Delete tool removes.

Classification objective

Try your own weights

Choose the number of hidden neurons and type the weights (x₃ = +1 is the bias input and f is the Heaviside step, f(g) = 1 for g ≥ 0). With 1 hidden neuron (one line) that neuron is also the output: y = f(ωh11x₁ + ωh12x₂ + ωh13·1). With 2 or more, an output neuron s1 combines them: y = f(ωs11y₁ + … + ωs1(m+1)·1). The lab tests the weights on your points and shows every weighted sum g.
