K-means lab

K-means sorts points into k groups by repeating two steps: assign every point to its nearest centroid, then move each centroid to the middle of its points. Press the big button under Run and watch what each step does.

Centroid
The centre of one group, drawn as a numbered circle.
Inertia
The total squared distance from every point to its own centroid. Lower means tighter groups.

Guided tasks

  1. Watch one loop

    Goal: predict where a centroid moves in the Move step, and say why.

    1. Press Assign points. Each point takes the colour and shape of its nearest centroid.
    2. Before you go on, point at where you expect each centroid to land.
    3. Press Move centroids and compare.

    Where does each centroid move?

  2. Run until it stops

    Goal: recognise when k-means has converged, and say why it stops.

    1. Press Play to watch every step, or Skip to end.
    2. Look at the inertia chart. It falls, then goes flat. It never goes up.
    3. Read the message under the canvas when the status turns to Converged.

    Why does the algorithm stop?

  3. Does the start matter?

    Goal: show that different starting centroids can give different results, and pick the best one.

    1. Press New random start, then Skip to end.
    2. Repeat about six times. Each finished run is added below.
    3. Look at a run with high inertia. How many centroids ended up in one blob?

      Some runs end with higher inertia. Which result do you keep?

    • Choose k

      Goal: pick k from the inertia-versus-k curve, and say why the lowest inertia is not enough.

      1. Press Plot inertia for k = 1 to 6. For each k the lab tries 10 random starts and keeps the lowest inertia.
      2. Look for the bend: the k after which extra clusters barely help.
      3. Load One cloud and plot again. Is the bend as clear as before?

      the k on the slider

      Why not just pick the k with the lowest inertia?

    • Find where it breaks

      Goal: name data shapes where k-means fails, and say why.

      1. Press Skip to end. Does each colour follow one moon?
      2. Keep Show regions on and look at the borders between the shaded areas.
      3. Load Two rings and try again.

      Why does k-means cut across these shapes?
