The update rule

v ← w·v + c₁·r₁·(pbest − x) + c₂·r₂·(gbest − x)
x ← x + v
  • Inertia – keep going the way you were going.
  • Cognitive – get pulled toward the best spot you have found.
  • Social – get pulled toward the best spot anyone has found.
  • r₁, r₂ are fresh random numbers in [0, 1] each step; they keep the swarm from moving in lockstep.

Inspect a particle

Click any particle on the map. Its three forces will be drawn as arrows from the particle after the next step, and their sizes listed here.

Try this

  • Pick Rastrigin and use Loners: with no social pull, particles never share information and the result is poor.
  • Use Herd follower: the swarm collapses fast, often on a local minimum. That is premature convergence.
  • Set w above 1: velocities grow and the swarm never settles.
  • On Himmelblau, watch the swarm choose between four equally good valleys.
